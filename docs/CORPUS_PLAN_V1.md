# PTAR Corpus Plan V1

Three distinct corpus classes are mandatory.

1. HISTORICAL
Recovered aggregate facts from the old project. Original assets/harness are missing.

2. CORE-PROBES
PTAR-CORE-PROBES-V1. Deterministic analytic directional stencils for algebra,
epsilon sensitivity, monotonicity and regression. Not an image-quality corpus.

3. PERCEPTUAL-V1
Permanent image acceptance corpus. Assets must be immutable and hashed.
Required families: natural photography, foliage/fur/hair-like detail, thin geometry,
oblique edges, high-contrast UI/text, raster/pixel art, repeated patterns,
curves, gradients and mixed-frequency textures.

No class may inherit scores from another class.
