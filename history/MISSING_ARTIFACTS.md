# Missing Historical Artifacts

Status date: 2026-08-17

The following artifacts are known to have existed or to have been required by the historical PTAR benchmark process,
but are not currently available in the accessible project storage:

- original EDGE corpus assets;
- original horse corpus assets;
- original naturel corpus assets;
- original RASTER corpus assets;
- original PTAR benchmark harness used for the historical aggregate results;
- exact original per-case score tables, unless later recovered;
- exact original NIS45 benchmark wrapper/configuration, unless later recovered.

This is not treated as a reason to rewrite history.

The historical aggregate results remain recorded as recovered project facts, but are explicitly marked as lacking their original
source artifacts.

If any of the above files are later recovered, they must be added through the recovery procedure in `docs/CORPUS_GOVERNANCE.md`.
