PTAR RC13 — FGQUALITY1 HARDWARE PROTOCOL

TARGET
Windows 8.1 x64 / GTX 960M / Warhammer 40,000: Inquisitor - Martyr
Game source: 1280x720
Native presenter: 1920x1080 / 60 Hz
For this RC13 quality test keep the in-game V-Sync ON. Auto-VSync is not implemented yet.

1. Close the game and launcher completely.
2. Install RC13 with 01-INSTALL_FULLSTACK1.bat, then run VERIFY_FULLSTACK1_INSTALL.bat. It must PASS and report Guard35 / profile 50 / FGQUALITY1.
3. Launch the same reproducible scene used for the RC12 deformation screenshots.
4. Keep FG OFF for 10 seconds. Confirm the game is NOT locked to 30 FPS by PTAR while FG is OFF.
5. Press CTRL+F6 once, release both keys fully, then move the character continuously for 20-30 seconds. Use lateral/diagonal movement that previously produced the strongest stretching.
6. Press F8 while FG remains ON.
7. Decisive status: P1FG7N ME SIZE must be 640x368. Target cadence remains approximately SOURCE 30 / DISPLAY 59-60 / GENERATED 29-30 / REAL DISPLAY 29-30. A small number of synthetic drops is acceptable if the higher ME workload needs protection; real-frame priority must remain intact.
8. During movement press F9 4 to 8 times, spaced over several seconds. Compare the character silhouette, legs, weapon/cape and newly revealed ground against RC12. Primary success criterion is visibly reduced rubber/stretch deformation. A modest local blend/ghosting is preferable to severe geometric stretching.
9. Press CTRL+F6 once to turn FG OFF. Confirm the PTAR 30 FPS governor releases immediately.
10. Do NOT test CTRL+F9 recorder/QSV in this run.
11. Close the game normally. Run diag\05-COLLECT_RESULTS.bat and send the result ZIP/folder plus the screenshots.

FAIL / STOP CONDITIONS
- crash/TDR;
- FG does not activate;
- ME SIZE remains 432x240;
- runtime failures or auto-disable become non-zero;
- display cadence collapses materially versus RC12;
- deformation becomes worse or broadens to the whole scene.

INTERPRETATION
If deformation drops materially with acceptable cadence, keep the /2 + Guard35 direction and tune from measured screenshots/logs. If cadence cost is excessive, the next step is an intermediate ME geometry or adaptive profile. If deformation remains despite 640x368, the limiting factor is likely occlusion/motion-boundary handling rather than raw ME resolution; do not keep increasing ME blindly.
