PTAR RC18 PRESENTDELIVERY1 DETERMINISM CONTRACT
===============================================
The patcher refuses any input whose SHA-256 differs from the exact RC17 field base.
It asserts both original pre-wait CALL rel32 byte sequences and targets, asserts the exact old Present argument bytes, and asserts the indirect Present call opcode.
It patches fixed RVAs only and recomputes the PE checksum.
The package validator rebuilds RC18 from diag\r\r070.dll and requires byte-for-byte identity with the shipped runtime.
