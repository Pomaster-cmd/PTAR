ROOT CAUSE
==========
GW16A only used the GW12 SOFT_FLAG to detect logical OFF. That flag is useful after a
CTRL+F6 ON->OFF transition, because GW12 LOADSHEDSOFT1 keeps the FG machinery internally
armed. But at process startup FrameGeneration=0, SOFT_FLAG has not yet been asserted.
The AUTO wrapper therefore sees startup OFF as an active AUTO session.

NEXT FIX — GW16B FGGATE1

- SOFT_FLAG == 0; and
- actual runtime FG-enabled state != 0.

When CTRL+F6 actually enables FG, AUTO initializes HIGH=60-visible target, then a heavy
scene may downshift after the required six >32 ms work samples.
