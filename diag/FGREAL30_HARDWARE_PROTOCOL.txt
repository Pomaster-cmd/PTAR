PTAR INQUISITOR RC5 FGREAL30 — HARDWARE PROTOCOL
================================================

Goal: isolate the new internal REAL half-rate governor. Do not test the recorder yet.

1. Close Inquisitor before installation.
2. Run 01-INSTALL_FULLSTACK1.bat.
3. Run VERIFY_FULLSTACK1_INSTALL.bat and require PASS.
4. Use Inquisitor at 1280x720 on the 60 Hz display; keep V-Sync enabled.
5. Start with FG OFF. Confirm the already validated baseline is still near 60 FPS.
6. Press CTRL+F6 once, release both keys, and play/move continuously for 30-60 s.
7. Expected source/HUD behavior: approximately 30 FPS while FG is ON.
8. Press F8 once after the FG state has been stable long enough to capture telemetry.
9. Check visually for crash/TDR, black frame, severe stutter, mouse offset, or temporal artifacts.
10. Press CTRL+F6 OFF. Source FPS must return quickly toward the ~60 FPS baseline.
11. Press F8 once after OFF, then optionally repeat one ON/OFF cycle.
12. Quit normally.
13. Run diag\05-COLLECT_RESULTS.bat and send the resulting ZIP.

Primary PASS target on 60 Hz:
- FG OFF source: ~60 FPS (same as RC4 field result)
- FG ON source REAL: ~30 FPS
- FG ON REAL display: near ~30/s
- FG ON GENERATED display: near ~30/s
- FG ON total display: near ~60/s
- FG OFF again: source returns toward ~60 FPS
- RUNTIME FAILURES / AUTO DISABLES: 0

Notes:
- The first REAL after FG activation is intentionally immediate.
- A source frame already later than the 30 FPS schedule is submitted immediately;
  FGREAL30 does not add a second 33 ms delay and never emits catch-up bursts.
- Recorder CTRL+F9 must remain OFF for this first governor validation.
