PTAR RC8 SHAREHR1 - INQUISITOR HARDWARE PROTOCOL
================================================
Purpose: capture the exact HRESULT returned by the producer CreateTexture2D failure already localized by RC6/RC7 to slot 0 / stage 1.

1. Keep Inquisitor at 1280x720. FG starts OFF.
2. Confirm normal uncapped/normal game framerate while FG is OFF.
3. Press CTRL+F6 once and fully release both keys.
4. Wait 2-3 seconds. The FG is expected to fail during this diagnostic run.
5. Press F8 once.
6. Quit normally and run diag\05-COLLECT_RESULTS.bat.
7. Return the result ZIP.

Expected diagnostic behavior:
- FG OFF remains free of the 30 FPS governor (GATE1 retained).
- If shared transport fails again at the same producer-texture call, P1FG7N LAST STATUS now contains the RAW 32-bit HRESULT instead of 0xF101.
- No NVENC/source submit is expected until this resource-creation incompatibility is fixed.

Do not use CTRL+F9 recorder during this run.
Auto-VSync is NOT implemented in RC8; its future contract remains strictly FG-only.
