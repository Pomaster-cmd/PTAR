Root cause in GW16C policy:

- HIGH then used a very tight fallback gate: 6 consecutive work samples >32 ms;
- a source around 33 FPS without FG has only about 30.3 ms/frame before FG overhead, GPU contention and scheduling variance;
- the 32 ms gate therefore leaves very little margin and collapses HIGH rapidly;
- after only 45 LOW source-frame backoff, the LOW branch again looks healthy and retries HIGH, producing repeated chatter.

- keep SAFELOWSTART1;
- HIGH fallback threshold becomes >34 ms;
- require 24 consecutive >34 ms work samples before fallback;
- after a failed HIGH attempt, wait 180 LOW source frames before another retry;
- initial LOW startup backoff remains 45 source frames;
- upshift qualification remains 45 consecutive <27 ms samples.

This is a hardware-validation experiment. It does not claim 60 FPS until externally measured.
