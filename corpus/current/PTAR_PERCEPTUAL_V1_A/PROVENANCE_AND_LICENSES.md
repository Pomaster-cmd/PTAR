# PTAR-PERCEPTUAL-V1-A provenance

Bundled scikit-image sample assets are included only where the official scikit-image
0.26 documentation identifies them as CC0 or public-domain/no known restrictions.

Included:
- brick — CC0
- camera — CC0
- grass — CC0
- gravel — CC0
- horse — CC0
- hubble_deep_field — NASA/public domain
- retina — CC0/public domain
- rocket — public domain

The `generated_*` sources are deterministic PTAR project assets created by the corpus builder.

The PNG `source_snapshots` are normalized snapshots of the arrays delivered by
scikit-image 0.26.0, not claims to be byte-identical copies of the upstream original files.
